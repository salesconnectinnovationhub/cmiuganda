Working Plan

Construction Working Process

01

Discuss with Us

In this initial phase, the project stakeholders engage in discussions to define the project scope, objectives, and requirements.

02

Planning

Once the initial discussions are complete, the planning process begins. This involves translating the gathered information into a comprehensive project plan.

03

Construction Starts

With the project plan in place and all necessary approvals obtained, the construction starts process begins.

04

Work Handover

The Work Handover process is the final phase where the constructed project is officially handed over to you.
