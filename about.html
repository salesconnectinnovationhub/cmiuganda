About Our Company

Building Dreams, Connecting Continents

Founded in 2018, CMI Group Co. (U) Limited bridges clients' architectural aspirations with the structural reality on the ground. We understand how complex it can feel to build a home or invest in property from far away.

We bring your plans together with engineering integrity, professional regulatory compliance, and transparent financial standards. Our remote-friendly process helps Ugandan diaspora clients stay informed from planning through handover.

Our mission is to deliver premium construction, property management, and engineering solutions that help diaspora clients and local developers build with confidence.

Company profile

A local team for projects that matter

CMI Group Co. (U) Limited was founded in 2018 to connect clients’ plans with construction and property work on the ground in Uganda. We work with local homeowners, developers, and diaspora clients who need a reliable way to plan, coordinate, and follow a project from a distance.

Our approach is to clarify the brief, agree the scope and costs before work begins, communicate progress, and carry out quality checks through handover. The exact services, milestones, reporting, and completion requirements are confirmed for each engagement.

Working Plan

Construction Working Process

01

Discuss with Us

In this initial phase, the project stakeholders engage in discussions to define the project scope, objectives, and requirements.

02

Planning

Once the initial discussions are complete, the planning process begins. This involves translating the gathered information into a comprehensive project plan.

03

Construction Starts

With the project plan in place and all necessary approvals obtained, the construction starts process begins.

04

Work Handover

The Work Handover process is the final phase where the constructed project is officially handed over to you.
