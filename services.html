How engagements work

Clear scope, practical deliverables

Each engagement is scoped to the site, service, and decisions you need to make.

01 · Understand the briefDiscuss goals, site information, constraints, and the service required.
02 · Agree scope and costReview the proposed work, relevant plans or quantities, exclusions, costs, and milestones before authorizing work.
03 · Deliver and reviewCoordinate agreed work, share progress updates, and review applicable quality checks and handover requirements.
Planning and estimates Where included in the agreed scope: plans, specifications, quantities, and a project-specific cost proposal. The wall estimator is an early quantity guide, not a quotation or full bill of quantities.
Construction and property work Deliverables, reporting frequency, schedule, and completion checks are set out for the particular project before work starts.

CMI does not publish a single rate card because project costs depend on scope, site conditions, specifications, quantities, location, and schedule. Request a written, project-specific estimate and confirm inclusions, exclusions, taxes, and payment milestones before committing.

Common questions

Before you get started

Can I manage a project while living abroad?

CMI describes a remote-friendly process with project communication and progress updates. Agree the update format, frequency, decision process, and named contacts in your project scope.

How do I get an accurate project cost?

Share the site location, project brief, plans if available, specifications, and expected timing. Ask for the scope, quantities, assumptions, exclusions, and payment milestones in a written proposal. The online wall estimator only approximates masonry-unit quantities.

How long will my project take?

Duration depends on the scope, site conditions, approvals, materials, and agreed delivery plan. Request a project-specific schedule and confirm how changes or delays are handled before work begins.

Are approvals, maintenance, and warranties included?

These are not universal inclusions. Confirm which approvals or services are in scope, and request any maintenance responsibilities and warranty terms in writing before signing.
