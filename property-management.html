Property management FAQs

Know what to agree before engagement

What does property management include?

Services can include property oversight, maintenance coordination, trustee support, or development coordination. Agree the exact tasks, authority limits, vendors, reporting, and fees in writing for your property.

How will I receive updates while abroad?

Agree your preferred channels, update frequency, records or photos required, and who can approve work or spending before the service starts.

How are maintenance costs approved?

Set an approval threshold, emergency contact process, documentation requirements, and payment method in your service agreement. Do not assume third-party maintenance costs are included in a management fee.

Is there a standard maintenance or warranty package?

No blanket package is published. Ask CMI to confirm the service duration, covered work, exclusions, response process, and any maintenance or warranty terms in writing.
