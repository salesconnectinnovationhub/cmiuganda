Pay securely with PesaPal

Use PesaPal to send funds to CMI Group for construction, property, or support payments. The payment is secure, the amount is confirmed before you complete the transaction, and confirmation is sent to your email or WhatsApp.

Before you pay:
  • Accepted currencies: UGX and USD, depending on the merchant configuration available at checkout.
  • Fees: processing fees are charged by PesaPal and may appear during checkout before confirmation.
  • Recipient: CMI Group Co. Ltd. Payment purpose should match the project or support request.
  • Confirmation: a payment confirmation is sent by email or WhatsApp after successful completion.
  • Support: contact us at cmi.groupuganda@gmail.com or +256 789 612 187 if the payment does not go through.
